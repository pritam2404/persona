6. "Prediction" kaise kaam karta hai

  1. Aaj jo skip ya adhoora raha → kal pehle (carry-over).
  2. Jo kaam 14 din mein 3+ alag din hua → routine, kal bhi.
  3. Time = us kaam ka asli median time. Agar tumhare kaam estimate se 20%+ lambe chalte hain to carry-over mein buffer.
  4. Focus kaam (Deep work, Study, Side project) → high-energy ghante (avg energy 3.5+).
  5. Baaki khali jagah mein, kabhi overlap nahi.

ML kyun nahi? 30 din se kam data pe ML pattern nahi, noise seekhta hai. Simple rules jinka kaaran dikhe, abhi zyada sahi hain. 30+ din data ke baad ML add kar sakte hain. Interview mein ye bolna strong point hai.

7. Brain se data?

Aaj possible nahi: EEG headband sirf rough "focused / relaxed" signal dete hain, ye nahi ki tum kya kar rahe ho. Practical agla step: smartwatch / phone ka neend, steps, heart rate import karke energy se match karna. App ka data model isi ke liye extendable hai.
