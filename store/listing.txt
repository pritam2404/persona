Full description (max 4000):
Persona helps you understand how your day really goes and plan a better tomorrow.

LOG YOUR DAY
Note each task in seconds: what you did, when, how long you expected it to take, your energy level and whether you finished it.

SEE YOUR PATTERNS
- Where your time goes: work vs personal, by category
- Your highest-energy hours
- How often tasks take longer than you planned
- Which tasks you keep skipping
Every insight shows the data behind it, so you can trust it.

A SMARTER PLAN FOR TOMORROW
Persona suggests tomorrow's schedule: unfinished tasks first, your routines included, realistic durations, and focus work placed in your best-energy hours. Each suggestion explains why.

YOUR COACH VIDEOS
Generate a short daily coach script in Hindi or English, and keep your coach videos in one place on your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your tasks and videos stay on your phone. Work task names are never included in coach scripts.
