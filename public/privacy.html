Persona - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Summary

Persona does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What the app stores

  • Tasks you log (title, times, category, energy, status, notes) are saved in your browser's local storage on your device.
  • Videos you add are saved in your device's browser storage (IndexedDB).
  • Your display name and language preference are saved on your device.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing is sent to the developer. The app has no server, no account, no analytics and no ads.
  • Files leave your device only when you choose to: Export (backup file), Script download, or Save / Share of a video to another app you pick.

Deleting your data

Delete individual tasks or videos inside the app, or clear the app's storage / uninstall the app to remove everything.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/pritam2404/persona.
